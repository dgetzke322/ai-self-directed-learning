What sections would a skilled Product Manager always include in a PRD that a developer needs before beginning implementation? 
Think beyond the feature list: non-functional requirements, success criteria, explicit out-of-scope items, and open questions.

Product Overview: What the product is and its purpose.
Goals and Objectives: What problem the product solves.
User Stories: How users will interact with the product.
Functional Requirements: Features and behaviors the product must have.
Non-Functional Requirements: Performance, reliability, security, accessibility, and usability requirements.
Success Metrics: How success will be measured.
Constraints and Assumptions: Technical or business limitations.
